Live Classes

Reserve a free seat for Midnimo Academy's crypto and finance lessons. Our pilot uses Jitsi Meet: the meeting opens in a new tab, with no extra student account required.

How to join a live lesson

1. Sign in with your Midnimo Academy student account. 2. Reserve a seat in a published class below. 3. When the teacher adds a meeting link, select Join Jitsi Meet. The meeting opens in a separate tab.

No extra student registration is needed for Jitsi. Your teacher will arrange the meeting and may ask to admit you. Only use links shared through your student dashboard.

Attendance policy

Attendance is required. Submit absence notice at least 2 hours before class. One unexcused absence may result in dismissal after administrator review. Emergencies may be considered.

Academy announcements

Pilot platform: Jitsi Meet. Teachers or administrators must publish a class and add its Jitsi meeting link before students can join. For longer lessons, teachers may provide separate meeting links. Reservation and website sign-in do not by themselves guarantee meeting admission or video capacity.
