MIDNIMO = UNITY

One community. Open knowledge.

Midnimo Academy is a free learning community for Somali youth. We teach practical skills with honesty and respect, without promising jobs, income or investment returns.

Our vision

A generation of Somali young people who understand money, technology and online markets and build a united community.

Our mission

Free, practical education in crypto, online markets and digital skills, accessible without fees that exclude learners.

Our promise

Honest teaching, no guaranteed profits, no handling student funds and no compulsory payment for core courses.

Frequently asked questions

Is Midnimo Academy free?

Yes. Basic membership and the main courses are free.

Will Midnimo Academy invest my money?

No. Midnimo Academy is not a bank, exchange or investment fund, and does not hold learners' funds.

Will I receive a job or guaranteed income?

No. Learning paths are educational and do not guarantee employment, income or investment returns.
