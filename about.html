About Midnimo Academy

Learn. Understand. Grow. / Baro. Faham. Koboc.

Our purpose

Midnimo Academy is an educational community focused on making practical knowledge accessible to Somali youth without charging for courses. Our subjects include financial literacy, cryptocurrency and financial markets, blockchain technology, and digital skills.

How learning works

Public course pages describe our curriculum. Registered students may use student services and join scheduled live learning sessions when available. A published course outline is not a promise that all lessons are already available.

Our approach

We prioritise accessible explanations, critical thinking, online safety, and responsible financial education. We do not guarantee jobs, income, or investment returns. Educational material is not personalised financial advice.

Our Story & Achievements

From protecting our community to building a learning movement. Midnimo Academy began approximately one year and three months ago with a simple mission: help Somali learners recognise scams, resist financial hype and fear of missing out (FOMO), and access trustworthy education without being pressured into expensive programmes.

We saw how misleading promises, aggressive marketing and get-rich-quick narratives could put learners at risk. Rather than adding to that noise, we chose to share knowledge freely, encourage independent thinking and build a supportive community. Our concern is with harmful practices, not a claim that every other school operates this way.

🎓 From Class 1 to Class 5

Over our first year and three months, we supported hundreds of students through Classes 1, 2, 3 and 4. We are now running Class 5, continuing our commitment to practical, accessible education.

🤝 Midnimo Membership

Graduates can continue learning together through Midnimo Membership. Members exchange ideas, support one another and discuss live trading activity in a collaborative environment. Trading involves risk, and shared discussions are educational rather than guaranteed signals or profits.

🎤 Midnimo Big Channel

Our wider community channel has nearly 2,700 members. We invite knowledgeable voices from the community to lead lectures, conversations and events covering diverse subjects and new perspectives.
