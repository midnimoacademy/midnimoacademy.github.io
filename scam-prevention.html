What if you have already been scammed?

Stop contact with the suspected scammer. Contact your bank or payment provider immediately using a verified channel, secure affected accounts, keep evidence and report the incident to the relevant authorities. Never pay someone who promises guaranteed recovery of stolen funds.

UK residents can find official reporting guidance at Report Fraud. For immediate danger, contact emergency services.
